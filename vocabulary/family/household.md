# Household

**householder**: `/ˈhaʊshəʊldə(r)/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who owns or rents a house or is responsible for it.

**breadwinner**: `/ˈbredwɪnə(r)/` - `n. [C]`  
The person who earns the money that a family needs to live.

**homemaker**: `/ˈhəʊmmeɪkə(r)/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who manages a home and takes care of the family, rather than earning money outside the home.

**dependant**: `/dɪˈpendənt/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who depends on someone else for financial support.
