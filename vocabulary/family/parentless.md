# Parentless

**orphan**: `/ˈɔːfn/` - `n. [C]`  
A child whose one or both parents have died; usage can vary by context.

**orphaned**: `/ˈɔːfnd/` - `adj.`  
Having lost one or both parents through death.

**orphanage**: `/ˈɔːfənɪdʒ/` - `n. [C]`  
A residential institution for children who have lost their parents or cannot be cared for by them.

**orphanhood**: `/ˈɔːfən hʊd/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being an orphan.
