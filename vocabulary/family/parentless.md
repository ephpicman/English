# Parentless

**orphan**: `/ˈɔːfn/` - `n. [C]`  
A child whose parents have died.

**orphaned**: `/ˈɔːfnd/` - `adj.`  
Having lost one or both parents through death.

**orphanage**: `/ˈɔːfənɪdʒ/` - `n. [C]`  
A residential institution for children whose parents have died or cannot care for them.

**orphanhood**: `/ˈɔːfən hʊd/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being an orphan.
