# Parentless

**orphan**: `/ˈɔːfn/` - `n. [C]`  
A child whose one or both parents have died; usage can vary by context.
  - The orphan was cared for by a family friend.
  - They often discuss a **war orphan**.
  - They often discuss an **orphaned child**.

**orphaned**: `/ˈɔːfnd/` - `adj.`  
Having lost one or both parents through death.
  - The children were orphaned when both their parents died.
  - She was **orphaned at a young age** and raised by her aunt.
  - Thousands of children were **left orphaned by the war**.

**orphanage**: `/ˈɔːfənɪdʒ/` - `n. [C]`  
A residential institution for children who have lost their parents or cannot be cared for by them.
  - He grew up in an orphanage before being adopted.
  - He had to **grow up in an orphanage** after losing his parents.
  - The children were **sent to an orphanage** when no relatives could care for them.

**orphanhood**: `/ˈɔːfən hʊd/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being an orphan.
  - The novel explores the loneliness and uncertainty of orphanhood.
  - The memoir describes what it was like to **experience orphanhood**.
  - They often discuss the **trauma of orphanhood**.
