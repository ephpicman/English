# Adoptive Family

**adoptive parent**: `/əˈdɒptɪv ˈpeərənt/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who legally becomes the parent of a child through adoption.

**biological parent**: `/ˌbaɪəˌlɒdʒɪkl ˈpeərənt/` - `n. [C]`  
A parent who is biologically related to a child.

**birth parent**: `/ˈbɜːθ peərənt/` - `n. [C]`  
A biological parent of an adopted child.

**birth mother**: `/ˈbɜːθ mʌðə(r)/` - `n. [C]`  
A woman who gives birth to a child, especially one who is later adopted.

**birth father**: `/ˈbɜːθ fɑːðə(r)/` - `n. [C]`  
A child's biological father, especially when the child is adopted.

**adopt**: `/əˈdɒpt/` - `v. [T]`  
To take another person's child legally into your family and become their parent.

**adopted**: `/əˈdɒptɪd/` - `adj.`  
Having become part of a family through adoption.

**adoptee**: `/ˌædɒpˈtiː/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who has been adopted.

**adoption**: `/əˈdɒpʃn/` - `n. [U]`  
The legal process of taking another person's child into your family.

**adoptive**: `/əˈdɒptɪv/` - `adj.`  
Related to adoption or to the family that adopts a child.
