# Parenthood

**co-parent**: `/ˌkəʊ ˈpeərənt/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who shares the responsibility of raising a child with another parent.

**single parent**: `/ˌsɪŋɡl ˈpeərənt/` - `n. [C]`  
A parent who brings up a child without a partner.

**single-parent family**: `/ˌsɪŋɡl ˈpeərənt ˌfæməli/` - `n. [C]`  
A family in which one parent brings up the children.

**two-parent family**: `/ˌtuː ˈpeərənt ˌfæməli/` - `n. [C]`  
A family in which a child is raised by two parents.

**paternal**: `/pəˈtɜːnl/` - `adj.`  
Related to someone's father or the father's side of a family.

**maternal**: `/məˈtɜːnl/` - `adj.`  
Related to someone's mother or the mother's side of a family.

**paternity**: `/pəˈtɜːnəti/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being someone's father.

**maternity**: `/məˈtɜːnəti/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being a mother.

**parental**: `/pəˈrentl/` - `adj.`  
Related to parents or to being a parent.

**motherhood**: `/ˈmʌðəhʊd/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being a mother.

**fatherhood**: `/ˈfɑːðəhʊd/` - `n. [U]`  
The state of being a father.

**parenting**: `/ˈpeərəntɪŋ/` - `n. [U]`  
The process of caring for children and helping them develop.

**upbringing**: `/ˈʌpbrɪŋɪŋ/` - `n. [C, U]`  
The way a child is cared for and taught while growing up.
