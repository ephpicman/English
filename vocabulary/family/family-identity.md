# Family Identity

**surname**: `/ˈsɜːneɪm/` - `n. [C]`  
The name shared by members of a family, usually written after a person's given name.
  - Please write your surname at the top of the form.

**maiden name**: `/ˈmeɪdn neɪm/` - `n. [C]`  
A woman's family name before she marries, especially if she changes it after marriage.
  - She kept her maiden name after getting married.

**birth name**: `/ˈbɜːθ neɪm/` - `n. [C]`  
The name a person was given at birth.
  - The actor later changed his birth name.

**family name**: `/ˈfæməli neɪm/` - `n. [C]`  
A surname shared by members of a family.
  - Both children have the same family name.

**given name**: `/ˈɡɪvn neɪm/` - `n. [C]`  
A personal name given to someone at birth or baptism, in addition to their family name.
  - Her given name is Elizabeth, but everyone calls her Liz.

**namesake**: `/ˈneɪmseɪk/` - `n. [C]`  
A person who has the same name as someone else, especially someone they are named after.
  - He was named after his grandfather and became his namesake.
