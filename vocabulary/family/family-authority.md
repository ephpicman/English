# Family Authority

**patriarch**: `/ˈpeɪtriɑːk/` - `n. [C]`  
The oldest or most powerful male member of a family.
  - As the family patriarch, he was often asked to settle disagreements.

**matriarch**: `/ˈmeɪtriɑːk/` - `n. [C]`  
The oldest or most powerful female member of a family.
  - The family matriarch organised the annual reunion.

**patriarchal**: `/ˌpeɪtriˈɑːkl/` - `adj.`  
Involving a system in which men hold most of the power in a family or society.
  - They challenged the patriarchal tradition that gave men all the decision-making power.
  - They live in a **patriarchal society**.
  - The novel challenges **patriarchal traditions**.

**matriarchal**: `/ˌmeɪtriˈɑːkl/` - `adj.`  
Involving a system in which women hold most of the power in a family or society.
  - In the matriarchal household, women held the main positions of authority.
  - The community has a **matriarchal structure**.
  - Researchers studied **matriarchal societies**.

## Idioms

**wear the trousers**
- **Meaning:** To be the person who has the most control or authority in a relationship or household.
- **Register:** Informal; especially British English.
- **Use:** Usually said about a couple or household. It can be humorous, critical, or based on traditional gender stereotypes.
- **Example:** Everyone assumes her husband makes the decisions, but she is the one who **wears the trousers** at home.
- **Caution:** Potentially old-fashioned or sexist in implication. Avoid it in sensitive professional discussions about relationships.
