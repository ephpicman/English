# Foster Family

**foster**: `/ˈfɒstə(r)/` - `v. [T]`  
  - They agreed to **foster a child** for six months.
  - The couple decided to **foster kittens** while the shelter was full.
To care for a child in your home, usually temporarily, without becoming the child's adoptive parent.
  - They agreed to foster a child until a permanent placement could be arranged.

**foster parent**: `/ˈfɒstə peərənt/` - `n. [C]`  
  - A foster parent provides **temporary care**.
  - The child was placed with **approved foster parents**.
A person who provides a home and care for a child who is not their own, usually temporarily.
  - A foster parent provided a safe home while the child's case was reviewed.

**foster child**: `/ˈfɒstə tʃaɪld/` - `n. [C]`  
  - The foster child needed a **stable home**.
  - She became close to her **foster family**.
A child who is being cared for by a foster parent or foster family.
  - The foster child stayed with the same family for two years.
