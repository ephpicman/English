The symbol `(r)` indicates that British pronunciation will have `/r/` only if a vowel sound follows directly at the beggining of the next word, as in **far awar**; otherwise the `/r/` is omitted.

`/x/` represents a fricative sound as in Scottish loch, Irish lough.

| IPA    | Sample | Full word IPA |
| ------ | ------ | ------------- |
| `/eɪ/` | say    | `/seɪ/`       |

| `/aɪ/` | my     | `/maɪ/`       |
| `/ɔɪ/` | boy    | `/bɔɪ/`       |
| `/aʊ/` | now    | `/naʊ/`       |
| `/ɪə/` | near   | `/nɪə(r)/`    |
| `/eə/` | hair   | `/heə(r)/`    |
| `/ʊə/` | pure   | `/pjʊə(r)/`   |

Nasalized vowels, marked with `/ ̃/`, may be retained in certain words taken from French, as in **penchant**.

## 4. Stress

`/'/`: **primary** stress

`/ˌ/`: **secondary** stress
