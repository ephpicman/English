# Improvement proposal: strengthen maintenance handovers

*Illustrative improvement proposal. Confirm current maintenance procedures and safety requirements before use.*

## Before you draft

The proposal targets information lost between shifts and must preserve urgent escalation procedures.

## Draft structure

It connects handover gaps to a standard record and a review process.

## Title and metadata

**Owner:** Facilities manager  
**Status:** Improvement proposal  
**Decision requested:** Approve a revised handover checklist

## Executive summary

Introduce a consistent handover checklist for open maintenance requests. The checklist should identify the location, current condition, work completed, remaining risk, responsible person, and next action. A short pilot across two shift handovers will test whether the format improves continuity.

## Current process and evidence of the problem

Maintenance updates are recorded in tickets and sometimes passed verbally between staff. The facilities manager should review recent examples where the next shift needed clarification or repeated a check to establish whether a standard handover would help.

## Root causes and constraints

The current process relies on staff remembering which details are important for each type of job. The checklist must remain concise and should not duplicate information already recorded in the official maintenance system.

## Recommended improvement

Use a short handover entry linked to the maintenance ticket. Require a current status, outstanding hazard or access issue, owner, next action, and escalation route. For urgent safety matters, staff must continue to follow the established emergency procedure immediately rather than wait for a routine handover.

## Implementation and change management

The facilities manager will draft the checklist with technicians, explain when it is required, and review completed entries after the pilot. Staff can suggest changes, but the final format should be approved by the manager.

## Benefits, costs, and risks

The change requires limited training and review time. It may reduce ambiguity, but a checklist cannot replace a site inspection or specialist judgment. Excessive duplication is a risk, so the pilot should remove fields that do not support safe continuity.

## Measures, review, and approval

Approve the pilot and review handover completeness, repeated clarification requests, and user feedback after two weeks.

## Language and quality review

Confirm that the checklist supports, rather than overrides, safety policy.

## IELTS-oriented practice note

The proposal states the scope, exception rules, and review criteria clearly.
