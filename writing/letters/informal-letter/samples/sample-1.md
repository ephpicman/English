# Informal letter: a personal update

*Illustrative personal letter. Adapt the details to your real relationship and experience.*

## Plan first

Share the update, respond warmly, and suggest a natural next step.

## Letter structure

Keep the tone friendly and specific rather than turning the note into a formal report.

## Friendly opening

Hi Maya,

How have you been? I hope the new job is settling down and you’re getting a chance to explore the neighbourhood.

## Reason for writing

I wanted to tell you about the photography walk I joined last weekend. I thought it would be a quick stroll, but we ended up following a quiet canal path and taking pictures of the old bridges and small cafés.

## Main details and personal response

I was surprised by how much I enjoyed slowing down and noticing details I usually walk past. One of the group members showed me how to use reflections in the water, and it completely changed the pictures I took. I’m still learning, but it was a lovely way to spend a Saturday morning.

## Suggestions, plans, or questions

You should come along next time if you’re free. The group usually shares the route a few days beforehand, so we can choose a shorter walk if the weather is bad. Let me know which weekends suit you.

## Warm closing and sign-off

Anyway, I’d better get back to my errands. Write soon and tell me how things are going.

Take care,  
Jordan

Check that the details and suggested plans reflect the actual relationship and circumstances.
