# Informal letter: a personal update

*Illustrative personal letter. Adapt the details so they reflect your real relationship and experience.*

## Plan first

The purpose is to share a personal update, respond warmly, and suggest a natural next step.

## Letter structure

Keep the tone friendly and specific; avoid turning a personal note into a formal report.

## Friendly opening

Hi Maya,

## Reason for writing

I wanted to tell you about the little photography walk I joined last weekend. I thought it would be a quick stroll, but we ended up exploring a quiet canal path and taking pictures of old bridges and reflections.

## Main details and personal response

The best part was that nobody rushed to get the perfect shot. We compared angles, noticed details we would normally walk past, and stopped for coffee when it started to rain. I came home with a few pictures I actually like, which is rare for me!

## Suggestions, plans, or questions

You would enjoy it because you always notice colours and patterns. Would you like to go together next Saturday? We could meet near the station around 10 and bring our cameras or phones.

## Warm closing and sign-off

Let me know if you’re free. It would be lovely to catch up. Take care, Alex

## Final review

Check that the details sound natural for the relationship, the invitation or question is clear, and the closing matches the level of familiarity.
