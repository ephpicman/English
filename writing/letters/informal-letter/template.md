## Plan first

1. List every point requested in the prompt and allocate each to a paragraph.
2. Match the tone to the relationship. Informal does not mean careless; formal does not mean unnecessarily complicated.
3. Put the main purpose early and support it with relevant details.
4. Use a clear topic for each paragraph and natural links between ideas.
5. Prefer precise everyday English to forced idioms or memorized expressions.
