## Plan first

1. Read the prompt and list every point that must be covered.
2. Identify the recipient and choose the right register: formal, semi-formal, or informal.
3. Decide the purpose of each paragraph before writing.
4. Put the reason for writing near the beginning; keep background relevant and requests specific.
5. Prefer natural, precise language. Avoid memorized phrases, unnecessary idioms, and excessively long sentences.

## IELTS and language review

- All required bullet points are covered with enough detail.
- The tone is appropriate to the recipient and consistent throughout.
- Paragraphs are logically ordered and each has a clear purpose.
- Vocabulary is precise and varied without sounding forced.
- Check verb tense, articles, prepositions, subject–verb agreement, punctuation, and sentence boundaries.
- The closing matches the greeting and does not introduce an unrelated point.
- Word count meets the task requirement; all placeholders and drafting notes are removed.
