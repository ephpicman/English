# Formal letter: Enquiry about a training programme

*Illustrative letter. Verify programme dates, eligibility, and application requirements.*

## Plan first

The letter asks clear questions and provides relevant context without assuming eligibility.

## Letter structure

Identify the programme and request the information needed to decide whether to apply.

## Address, date, subject, and greeting

Samira Khan  
26 River Street  
Berlin 10115  
24 October 2026

**Subject: Enquiry about the evening project-management course**

Dear Training Coordinator,

## Purpose and main message

I am writing to request further information about your evening certificate course in project management.

## Necessary background

I currently assist with scheduling and action tracking and would like to develop my understanding of project planning, risk management, and stakeholder communication.

## Explanation and supporting points

I would be grateful for the next course dates, weekly timetable, total fee, and entry requirements. I would also like to know whether participants complete a practical project and whether the course is available in person or online.

## Requested action and timing

As I work full time, evening sessions would be most suitable. If the next intake is full, information about the following intake would also be helpful.

## Formal closing and sign-off

Thank you for your assistance. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Samira Khan
