Jordan Lee  
18 Cedar Walk

**Subject: Request for accessible public meeting information**

Dear Sir or Madam,

## Purpose and main message

I am writing to ask that the public information for the upcoming neighbourhood meeting include clear details about access to the venue.

## Necessary background

Some attendees may need to know in advance whether the entrance is step-free, whether accessible seating is available, and whom to contact if they have an access question. These details help people decide whether they can attend and make any necessary arrangements.

## Explanation and supporting points

The current notice gives the date and location but does not describe the entrance or available facilities. I do not wish to assume that the venue is inaccessible; I would simply appreciate confirmation of the arrangements so the information can be accurate.

## Requested action and timing

Could you please provide the relevant access details and update the notice if appropriate? A reply before the notice is circulated again would be helpful.

## Formal closing and sign-off

Thank you for your assistance. I look forward to your response.

Yours faithfully,  
Jordan Lee
