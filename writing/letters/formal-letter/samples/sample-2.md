# Formal letter: Request for a review of a parking decision

*Illustrative formal letter. Verify the decision reference, dates, and appeal process before sending.*

## Plan first

The letter requests a review and provides relevant facts without making unsupported legal claims.

## Letter structure

Identify the decision, explain the concern, and state the action requested.

## Address, date, subject, and greeting

Alex Turner  
42 Linden Road  
Berlin 10117  
24 October 2026

**Subject: Request for review of parking notice PN-4821**

Dear Sir or Madam,

## Purpose and main message

I am writing to request a review of parking notice PN-4821, issued on 17 October 2026.

## Necessary background

The notice states that the vehicle was parked outside the permitted period on Linden Road. I would like the record to be checked because the displayed time on the notice appears to differ from the time shown on my parking receipt.

## Explanation and supporting points

I have retained the receipt and a copy of the notice. I understand that the original record may contain additional information, so I am not assuming that the notice was issued incorrectly. I am asking for the details to be compared before I decide whether any further information is needed.

## Requested action and timing

Please confirm the process for submitting the receipt and advise whether the notice can be reviewed before the payment deadline. If a formal appeal form is required, please direct me to the correct procedure.

## Formal closing and sign-off

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

Yours faithfully,  
Alex Turner
