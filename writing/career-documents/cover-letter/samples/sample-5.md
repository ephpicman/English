# Application for Sustainability Project Assistant

*Illustrative cover letter. Verify the project focus and the applicant’s qualifications before use.*

## Opening and target role

I am applying for the Sustainability Project Assistant position at Greenway Facilities. My experience coordinating small projects, gathering information from multiple sources, and preparing concise progress updates would help me support practical sustainability initiatives.

## Employer-specific motivation

I am interested in work that turns broad environmental goals into actions that can be planned and measured. I would value the chance to help teams track progress while reporting limitations honestly.

## Relevant qualification

My studies introduced me to resource efficiency, waste reduction, and basic project evaluation. In a volunteer role, I helped coordinate a reuse event, maintained a list of donated items, and prepared a short summary of what was collected and distributed. I am comfortable organising records and following up with people to confirm missing details.

## Achievement evidence

During the reuse event, the first collection sheet mixed item counts with estimates of weight, making it difficult to compare results. I suggested separating measured values from estimates and adding a note about the method used. The revised summary was easier to interpret and made clear which figures could be compared with future events. I learned that sustainability reporting depends not only on collecting numbers but also on explaining how they were obtained and what they do not establish.

## Fit and contribution

I would bring careful documentation, practical coordination, and a willingness to ask how success should be measured before a project begins. I am keen to learn from colleagues with technical expertise.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my organisational skills could support your sustainability projects.
