# Application response: Describe a time you improved a process

*Illustrative response; adapt the example to the applicant’s actual experience and the employer’s word limit.*

## Direct answer to prompt

In an administrative role, I helped improve how our team tracked incoming requests. Previously, requests arrived through email and a shared spreadsheet, which made it difficult to see who owned each item and when it needed attention.

## Relevant context

The team was small, and introducing a complicated new system would have taken time away from daily work. The goal was to make the existing process easier to use, not to add unnecessary reporting.

## Evidence of capability

I reviewed a sample of recent requests and asked colleagues what information they needed when taking over an item. I proposed one tracker with a reference number, status, owner, due date, and next action. I transferred active requests, checked unclear entries with their owners, and wrote a short guide explaining how to update the tracker. During the first weekly review, I asked colleagues to point out fields that were confusing or unnecessary.

## Outcome and relevance

The team gained a clearer view of responsibility and could identify overdue requests during the weekly review. The change also made handovers easier because the next action was visible in one place. I learned that a process improvement works best when users help shape it and responsibilities for maintaining it are explicit. This experience is relevant to roles that require coordination, careful records, and practical problem-solving.

## Closing if useful

I would bring the same consultative, measured approach to improving processes in this role.
