# Application response: Describe a time you improved a process.

*Illustrative application response. Adapt the example to the applicant’s real experience and the employer’s exact word limit.*

## Direct answer to prompt

In a part-time administrative role, requests arrived through email and a shared spreadsheet, so two colleagues sometimes worked on the same item while other requests were overlooked.

## Relevant context

The situation required me to balance practical constraints with the needs of colleagues who depended on accurate information. I clarified what success would look like before choosing an approach.

## Evidence of capability

I first reviewed a sample of recent requests and asked colleagues which information they needed at handover. I proposed one tracker with a unique reference, status, owner, and next action. I transferred active items, checked unclear entries with their owners, and wrote a short guide explaining how to update the tracker.

## Outcome and relevance

The team had a clearer view of ownership and could identify overdue requests during its weekly review. The example shows how I use observation and consultation before changing a process, rather than introducing a tool without understanding the work.

## Closing if useful

I would apply the same measured approach to improving processes in this role.
