# Application response: Tell us about a time you handled competing priorities

*Illustrative response; replace the scenario with a truthful example from the applicant’s experience.*

## Direct answer to prompt

When two urgent tasks arrived close together during a busy service shift, I clarified their deadlines and the consequences of delay before deciding how to proceed.

## Relevant context

One task required a same-day response to a customer, while the other involved preparing documents for a meeting the following morning. Both mattered, but they had different levels of urgency and different people depending on them.

## Evidence of capability

I checked the customer’s request to establish what information was needed for a complete answer and estimated how long the document preparation would take. I informed my supervisor of the overlap and confirmed that another colleague could review the meeting documents once I had assembled them. I then sent the customer a clear acknowledgement with a realistic response time, completed the document pack, and returned to the customer enquiry within the agreed window. I recorded the status of both tasks so that neither disappeared from view.

## Outcome and relevance

Both tasks were completed within their required timeframes, and the customer knew when to expect a full response. The experience taught me not to equate the loudest request with the highest priority. I use deadlines, impact, dependencies, and communication to make decisions, and I raise conflicts early when they cannot be resolved independently.

## Closing if useful

I would apply this approach to balancing deadlines while keeping colleagues and customers informed.
