## Purpose and key message

Approve a two-cycle pilot of a short validation checklist for monthly reports.

## Background and current position

Reports are assembled from multiple source files, and checks vary by author. The team should review recent corrections to identify the most common error types and confirm that a shared checklist addresses them.

## Options or analysis

**Option A: Rely on existing author checks.** Minimal effort, but the approach may remain inconsistent.

**Option B: Introduce a short shared checklist.** Adds a review step but can make the reporting period, source version, and key totals easier to verify.

**Option C: Require a separate approval meeting for every report.** Provides additional scrutiny but may create delays disproportionate to the risk.

## Recommendation and rationale

Pilot Option B, with escalation to a specialist review where required. The checklist should not replace mandatory financial or compliance controls.

- Report authors: test it in the next two cycles.
- Managers: review effectiveness and workload by 15 December.

Verify the reporting rules and existing controls before circulation.
