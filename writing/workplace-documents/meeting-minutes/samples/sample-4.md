# Meeting minutes: monthly reporting quality review

## Meeting metadata and attendees

- **Date:** 21 October 2026
- **Facilitator:** Reporting team lead
- **Attendees:** Report authors, finance partner, operations analyst
- **Record type:** Illustrative minutes; verify with the actual meeting record

## Purpose / agenda

Review recent reporting corrections and agree whether a common pre-publication check is needed.

## Discussion summary by topic

The team reviewed examples of date-range mismatches and inconsistent source versions. Participants agreed that the review process varies by author. The finance partner noted that mandatory financial controls must remain separate from any new editorial checklist.

## Decisions made

The team agreed to draft a short checklist for two reporting cycles. The checklist will cover reporting period, source version, key totals, and confirmation that partial periods are labelled.

## Open issues and next meeting

The team has not yet established whether the checklist will reduce errors enough to justify the added review time. A follow-up meeting will be scheduled after two reporting cycles to review evidence and workload.
