# Urban green spaces: problem solution essay

*Illustrative IELTS-style essay. Adapt the argument to the exact question and required word count.*

## Before writing

Identify the causes of the problem and match each proposed solution to a cause.

## Essay structure

The essay explains the problem before presenting practical responses.

## Introduction and problem framing

Urban green spaces has become an important issue because it affects how people access services and organise everyday life. Addressing it requires identifying the main causes rather than relying on a single broad solution.

## Main problem or cause 1

Some neighbourhoods have few safe, welcoming places for residents to walk, play, or meet outdoors.

## Main problem or cause 2 (if needed)

Existing parks may be poorly maintained or difficult to reach safely, limiting their value to nearby residents.

## Solutions matched to causes

Local authorities should map gaps in access, improve safe walking routes, and maintain existing parks before creating new sites without a long-term plan. Smaller green spaces, tree-lined routes, and shared courtyards can complement larger parks in dense areas.

## Conclusion

The issue can be managed more effectively when the response addresses both the immediate barriers and the conditions that allow them to continue. Local authorities should map gaps in access, improve safe walking routes, and maintain existing parks before creating new sites without a long-term plan. Smaller green spaces, tree-lined routes, and shared courtyards can complement larger parks in dense areas.
