# Remote work: problem solution essay

*Illustrative IELTS-style essay. Adapt the argument to the exact question and required word count.*

## Before writing

Identify the causes of the problem and match each proposed solution to a cause.

## Essay structure

The essay explains the problem before presenting practical responses.

## Introduction and problem framing

Remote work has become an important issue because it affects how people access services and organise everyday life. Addressing it requires identifying the main causes rather than relying on a single broad solution.

## Main problem or cause 1

One challenge is that remote employees may miss informal information and mentoring that colleagues exchange in person.

## Main problem or cause 2 (if needed)

A second problem is the difficulty of separating work from personal time when the home becomes the workplace.

## Solutions matched to causes

Teams can document decisions, schedule mentoring and collaboration time, and agree on reasonable response windows. Managers should assess output and quality rather than online presence, while offering office access when it materially helps the work.

## Conclusion

The issue can be managed more effectively when the response addresses both the immediate barriers and the conditions that allow them to continue. Teams can document decisions, schedule mentoring and collaboration time, and agree on reasonable response windows. Managers should assess output and quality rather than online presence, while offering office access when it materially helps the work.
