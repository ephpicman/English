# Remote work: compare contrast essay

*Illustrative IELTS-style essay. Adapt the argument to the exact question and required word count.*

## Before writing

Use the same criteria to compare both options and avoid describing one in detail while barely discussing the other.

## Essay structure

The comparison is organised by shared criteria rather than two unrelated lists.

## Introduction and comparison basis

The choice between different approaches to remote work depends on what people need and which constraints matter most. This essay compares the options through flexibility, access, and practical limitations.

## Criterion 1: both subjects

Remote work gives employees flexibility over location and can reduce commuting, while office-based work makes spontaneous discussion and shared equipment easier to access.

## Criterion 2: both subjects

Remote arrangements rely on deliberate documentation and scheduled contact; office teams can exchange information informally but may face more interruptions and travel time.

## Synthesis and significance

The meaningful comparison is not simply comfort versus discipline, but which environment best supports the task, the employee, and the team’s communication needs.

## Conclusion

Neither option is automatically best in every setting. The more suitable choice depends on the intended outcome, available resources, and the needs of the people affected.

## Final IELTS checklist

Make comparisons explicit, use parallel criteria, and ensure the conclusion synthesises rather than repeats the introduction.
