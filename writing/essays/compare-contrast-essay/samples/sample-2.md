# Public transport investment: compare contrast essay

*Illustrative IELTS-style essay. Adapt the argument to the exact question and required word count.*

## Before writing

Use the same criteria to compare both options and avoid describing one in detail while barely discussing the other.

## Essay structure

The comparison is organised by shared criteria rather than two unrelated lists.

## Introduction and comparison basis

The choice between different approaches to public transport investment depends on what people need and which constraints matter most. This essay compares the options through flexibility, access, and practical limitations.

## Criterion 1: both subjects

Public transport carries multiple passengers along shared routes and can reduce the need for parking, while private cars provide flexible point-to-point travel.

## Criterion 2: both subjects

Transit depends on reliable schedules and convenient connections; cars depend on road access, parking, and the ability to absorb traffic and fuel costs.

## Synthesis and significance

The best system combines dependable transit with safe roads and realistic alternatives for areas that cannot be served efficiently by frequent public transport.

## Conclusion

Neither option is automatically best in every setting. The more suitable choice depends on the intended outcome, available resources, and the needs of the people affected.

## Final IELTS checklist

Make comparisons explicit, use parallel criteria, and ensure the conclusion synthesises rather than repeats the introduction.
