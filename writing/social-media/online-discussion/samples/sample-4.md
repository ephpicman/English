# Online discussion: Is customer feedback always reliable?

*Illustrative response; adapt it to the actual service or product being discussed.*

## Direct response

Customer feedback is valuable, but it should be treated as evidence to investigate rather than a complete picture of service quality.

## Reasoning and support

Feedback can reveal confusing instructions, repeated product problems, or gaps between what a service promises and what customers experience. However, people who respond may not represent every user, and a single complaint may reflect an unusual situation. Teams need to examine patterns and context before deciding what to change.

## Example or qualification

If several customers report difficulty completing the same form, the team can test the wording and observe users attempting the task. If one person reports a rare technical error, support staff should still investigate it without assuming it affects everyone.

## Engage another view

It is understandable to prioritise customer comments because they describe real experiences. The caution is not to dismiss them, but to combine them with case records, usability testing, and service data.

## Synthesis or question

The strongest decisions come from listening carefully and checking what the feedback shows.
