# Online discussion: Are online courses enough for professional development?

*Illustrative response to a discussion prompt. Adjust it to the actual context and other participants’ contributions.*

## Direct response

Online courses can be valuable, but they are rarely enough on their own for a skill that must be used reliably at work.

## Reasoning and support

A course provides explanation and examples, while practical tasks reveal whether a learner can transfer the idea to a less familiar situation. Feedback helps correct mistakes before they become habits.

## Example or qualification

After a course on data visualisation, a learner could redesign an existing report, explain the choices to a colleague, and revise it based on reader questions.

## Engage another view

Self-paced learning is accessible for many people and should not be dismissed. It works best when paired with practice and a realistic opportunity to use the skill.

## Synthesis or question

A good development plan combines learning material, application, feedback, and reflection. What has helped you turn a course into a lasting skill?
