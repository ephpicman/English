# Online discussion: Should teams automate routine tasks?

*Illustrative response; adapt it to the process and risks being discussed.*

## Direct response

Teams should automate routine tasks when the process is understood, the rules are stable, and exceptions can be handled safely.

## Reasoning and support

Automation can reduce repetitive work and make results more consistent. It can also create problems quickly if the underlying instructions are unclear or the data is unreliable. A team should first understand why a task is performed and what decisions depend on its output.

## Example or qualification

A standard reminder email may be suitable for automation if recipients, timing, and opt-out rules are well defined. A case that requires judgment about a customer’s circumstances may still need a trained person to review it.

## Engage another view

Those who advocate automating as much as possible are right that manual repetition can waste time. However, speed alone is not success if errors become harder to notice or correct.

## Synthesis or question

A small pilot with clear checks is a sensible way to test whether automation genuinely helps.
