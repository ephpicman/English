# A process is not improved until the people using it can explain what changed.

*Illustrative professional post; adapt it to a real experience and do not imply personal results you cannot verify.*

## Hook

A process is not improved until the people using it can explain what changed.

## Main point

When a workflow feels slow, it is tempting to add another tracker or approval step. Before changing the tool, map where requests pause, who needs to make a decision, and which information is missing.

## Support or experience

In a team exercise, we found that several delays came from unclear ownership rather than the software itself. Making the next owner and next action visible gave the team a better starting point for improvement.

## Practical takeaway

Pick one recurring handoff and ask: what must the next person know to continue without chasing context? Fix that gap before redesigning everything.

## Question or call to action

What small change has made a handoff easier in your team?
