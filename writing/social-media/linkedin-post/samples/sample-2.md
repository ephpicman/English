# Better meeting notes record decisions, not every sentence

*Illustrative professional post; adapt the recommendation to your team’s meeting style.*

## Hook

The most useful meeting note is the one that makes the next action obvious.

## Main point

A transcript can preserve what people said but still leave the team unsure what was agreed. Notes should distinguish decisions, open questions, and actions.

## Support or experience

For a project review, a short action table can record the task, owner, due date, and any dependency. Background discussion belongs in the notes only when it helps explain the decision. If a point remains unresolved, label it as open rather than writing it as though the team has agreed. This reduces the chance that a tentative suggestion becomes an accidental commitment.

## Practical takeaway

After the meeting, check each action with its owner and send the notes while the discussion is still fresh. At the next review, focus on actions that need a decision or escalation.
