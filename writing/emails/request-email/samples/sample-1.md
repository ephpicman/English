# Request for approval to attend a training course

*Illustrative workplace email; confirm course fees, dates, and internal policy before sending.*

## Plan before drafting

The request is specific, explains its relevance to current work, and gives the manager time to respond without assuming approval.

## Email structure

The message below separates the request, rationale, cost, and decision deadline.

## Subject line

Request for approval to attend accessible-document training

## Greeting and direct request

Hello Priya,

I would like approval to attend the one-day course on accessible document design on 19 November.

## Context and rationale

The course covers heading structure, readable layouts, and screen-reader navigation. These skills are directly relevant to the guidance documents I prepare for our team and could help us identify accessibility issues earlier.

## Specific details and constraints

The listed fee is €180, excluding travel. I have checked the team calendar and can complete the scheduled document review before the course. I will share a short summary of the useful techniques with colleagues afterwards.

## Deadline and flexibility

Could you let me know by 6 November so I can reserve a place? If the budget is unavailable this month, I would be happy to consider a later session.

## Thanks and sign-off

Thank you for considering the request.

Best regards,  
Jamie

## Final checklist

Verify the course details, price, and approval process before sending. Attach the course outline if the manager needs it to assess the request.

## IELTS-oriented language practice

The request is stated early, supported by a relevant reason, and softened with a realistic alternative rather than excessive apology.
