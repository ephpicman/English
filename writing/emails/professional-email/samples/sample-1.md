# Summary and actions from today’s review

*Illustrative professional email. Confirm recipients, deadlines, attachments, and status before sending.*

## Plan before drafting

The email has one clear purpose and identifies the action and timing required from the reader.

## Email structure

Keep the subject specific, put essential context before the request, and close with a clear next step.

## Subject line

Summary and actions from today’s review

## Greeting and purpose

Hello team, I am sharing a short summary of the review meeting and the actions agreed today.

## Essential context

We reviewed the current portal readiness status and confirmed that the content work is complete. Accessibility testing and the authentication dependency remain open.

## Main message or request

Please use the shared readiness checklist as the current source of status. If you identify a new blocker, add it to the risk section with an owner and next review date rather than creating a separate tracker.

## Action, timing, or next step

Morgan will confirm the authentication status by 5 November, and Priya will complete the accessibility review by 6 November. We will revisit the release decision at the readiness meeting on 7 November.

## Closing and sign-off

Thank you for keeping the checklist current. Best regards, Jordan

## Final checklist

Check the recipient, subject, dates, links, attachments, and any promise of follow-up. Make sure the message does not present a pending decision as approved.

## IELTS-oriented language practice

The email uses a clear purpose, concise context, and polite request. In real professional correspondence, accuracy and the reader’s next action matter more than elaborate phrases.
