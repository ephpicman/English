# Request to address late-night delivery noise

*Illustrative complaint; confirm dates and building procedures before sending.*

## Subject Line

Request to address late-night delivery noise

## Greeting and Purpose

Dear Building Manager, I am writing to report repeated noise from deliveries using the rear entrance late at night.

## Facts and Timeline

On Monday and Wednesday this week, delivery vehicles arrived after 11 p.m. and remained idling while crates were unloaded. The noise carried into bedrooms facing the courtyard. I have not spoken directly with the drivers and do not know whether the timing is part of an approved arrangement.

## Impact and Evidence

The disturbance has interrupted residents’ sleep on both nights. I understand deliveries may be necessary, but the late timing and unloading method appear to contribute to the problem. I can provide the dates and approximate times to help check building records.

## Requested Resolution

Could you contact the delivery operator to ask whether unloading can take place earlier or engines can be switched off while vehicles are stationary? Please let residents know what steps are feasible and when the arrangement can be reviewed.

## Closing

Thank you for looking into this. I would appreciate a response within five working days.

Sincerely,  
A resident
