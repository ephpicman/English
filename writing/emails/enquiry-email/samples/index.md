# Enquiry Email: Five Examples

Five original examples for **Enquiry Email**, written for the genre and audience implied by the corresponding template. Names, dates, and metrics are illustrative; adapt them to the real brief.

---

## Example 1: A university mentoring programme

**Subject:** Request to resolve a service issue

Dear Customer Support Team,

I am writing about a university mentoring programme. The service did not match the arrangements confirmed earlier, and my attempts to resolve the issue have not been successful. Please investigate and let me know whether a correction or refund is appropriate. I would appreciate an update within five working days.

Kind regards,
Alex Morgan

---

## Example 2: A missed project deadline

**Subject:** Following up on our discussion

Hello Priya,

I wanted to follow up regarding a missed project deadline. I appreciate that you have several priorities, so please let me know whether you have had a chance to review the information. I can send a concise summary or arrange a short call if that would be easier.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan

---

## Example 3: A new colleague

**Subject:** Enquiry about available options

Dear Hiring Team,

I am considering a new colleague and would be grateful if you could clarify the available options, expected timeline and total cost. Please also let me know whether any prerequisites or supporting documents are required. This will help me assess whether it meets our needs.

Yours faithfully,
Alex Morgan

---

## Example 4: A university course

**Subject:** Meeting and next steps

Hello everyone,

Could we meet on Thursday at 10:00 to discuss a university course? I suggest a 30-minute session covering the current position, open decisions and action owners. Please let me know by Tuesday if the time is unsuitable; I will circulate background notes in advance.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan

---

## Example 5: A workplace policy

**Subject:** Thank you for your support

Dear Jordan,

Thank you for your help with a workplace policy. Your prompt feedback made it easier to resolve the main questions and move forward with confidence. I particularly appreciated the clear explanation of the options. I will share the outcome with the wider team.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan
