# Meeting room availability for a workshop

*Illustrative enquiry; verify room requirements and dates before booking.*

## Subject

Meeting room availability for a workshop

## Greeting and context

Dear Facilities Team, I am organising an internal workshop and would like to check whether a suitable meeting room is available.

## Questions

Do you have a room for approximately 12 participants with a display screen and video-conferencing equipment? Could you confirm whether tables can be rearranged for group exercises and whether a whiteboard is available? Please also let me know if technical support is available at the start of the session and whether catering is permitted.

## Constraints

Our preferred date is 18 November, from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m., although the following afternoon would also work. We need step-free access for all attendees.

## Response request and sign-off

Please let me know which rooms may be suitable and whether a booking form or deposit is required.
