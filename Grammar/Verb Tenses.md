# Verb Tenses

## 1. Present Continuous

### Structure

```
Subject  +  am/is/are  +  verb  +  ing
```
**Note:** we usually use `'m`, `'s` and `'re` instead of `am`, `is` and `are` respectively.
```
am = 'm
is = 's
are = 're
```
**Examples:**
``` 
I            am  (= I'm)                  driving
he/she/it    is  (= he's/she's/it's)      working
We/you/they  are (=we're/you're/they're)  going
```

### When to use

usages
- When we started doing something and haven't finished yet (we are in the middle of doing it).
  **Examples:**
  - I'm trying to work.
  - He's having a shower.
  - Are you playing chess?
  **Notes:**
  - Sometimes the action is not happening at the time of speaking:
    _Examples:_
      - I'm reading a new book at the moment
      - I want to work in the UK, so I'm learning English.
- When we talk about a change  that has been started to happen (but not finished).
    _Examples:_
    - My Egnlish is getting better.
    - The population of the world is increasing very fast.
    - At first, I didn't like this course, but I'm starting to enjoy it now.

### Adverb of Time

We can use present continuous with periods around **now**:
- now
- today
- this week
- this year

**Examples:**
- You're working hard today.
- I am improving my English skills this year.
