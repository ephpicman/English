# IELTS General Training Writing Task 2 — Discussion Essay

## Practice prompt

Some people think that young adults should leave their family home as soon as they can afford to do so. Others believe that living with parents for longer is a better choice. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Write at least 250 words. You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

## Model answer

The age at which young adults should move out of the family home is often debated. While independent living can encourage maturity, remaining with parents may offer practical and emotional advantages. In my view, neither option is inherently better; the decision should depend on a person’s finances, responsibilities and readiness for independence.

Those who support moving out early argue that managing a household teaches skills that are difficult to acquire otherwise. Young adults must budget for rent and bills, prepare meals, and resolve everyday problems without relying on their parents. Living independently may also help them develop confidence and establish routines that reflect their own priorities. In addition, students or workers who move to another city may gain access to educational or professional opportunities that would otherwise be unavailable.

On the other hand, living with parents for longer can be a sensible response to high housing costs and insecure employment. Rather than spending most of their income on rent, young adults may be able to save for further education, repay debt or build a deposit for a home. This arrangement can benefit parents as well, particularly when household tasks and expenses are shared fairly. It may also provide stability during a difficult transition, such as starting a first job or completing a demanding course. However, these advantages are less convincing if the arrangement prevents a young adult from making decisions or contributing responsibly.

Ultimately, I believe that the quality of the arrangement matters more than the age at which someone leaves home. A young person who lives with parents but pays an agreed share of expenses and takes responsibility for daily tasks may be more independent than someone who lives alone but relies entirely on financial support. Conversely, moving out can be valuable when it creates opportunities for growth that are not available at home.

In conclusion, both choices have legitimate benefits. Young adults should aim for financial and personal responsibility, whether they achieve it inside the family home or by establishing a household of their own.

## Why this is a strong response

- **Task Response:** It discusses both viewpoints and gives a clear, nuanced opinion, as required.
- **Coherence and Cohesion:** Each body paragraph develops one side; the final paragraphs synthesize the comparison rather than merely repeat it.
- **Lexical Resource:** Expressions such as “insecure employment,” “a deposit for a home,” and “the quality of the arrangement” are relevant and precise.
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** Contrast clauses, conditional structures, relative clauses and varied sentence patterns are used to express nuanced relationships.
