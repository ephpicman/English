## How to use these samples

- Read the prompt and identify every instruction before studying the model answer.
- Notice how each response fulfils the task, organizes ideas, uses precise vocabulary, and varies sentence structures accurately.
- For Task 1, practise covering every bullet point and matching the tone to the recipient. For Task 2, practise developing each main idea with explanation and a relevant example or consequence.
- After writing your own response, compare it with the sample using the four criteria below.

## Self-review rubric

| Criterion | What to check |
|---|---|
| Task Achievement (Task 1) / Task Response (Task 2) | Every instruction is addressed; the purpose or position is clear; ideas are sufficiently developed. |
| Coherence and Cohesion | Paragraphs follow a logical order; each has a clear purpose; linking is natural and references are clear. |
| Lexical Resource | Vocabulary is accurate, varied, and natural; collocations and word forms are controlled. |
| Grammatical Range and Accuracy | Sentence structures vary; complex forms are controlled; grammar and punctuation errors are rare and do not impede meaning. |

The samples are designed to demonstrate high-band characteristics, but they are not official IELTS answers and do not guarantee a particular band score. Only a qualified examiner can provide an official assessment. Word counts are approximate; always follow the instructions in your actual test.
