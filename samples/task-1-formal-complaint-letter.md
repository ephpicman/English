# IELTS General Training Writing Task 1 — Formal Complaint Letter

## Practice prompt

You recently stayed at a hotel and were dissatisfied with the service. Write a letter to the hotel manager. In your letter:

- explain when you stayed and why you chose the hotel;
- describe the problems you experienced;
- say what you would like the manager to do.

Write at least 150 words. You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

## Model answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to complain about my stay at the Riverside Hotel from 12 to 15 September, which I booked through your website because it advertised quiet rooms and reliable internet access.

Unfortunately, the service fell well below the standard I had expected. On the first night, loud music from a private event continued until nearly 1 a.m., despite my calling reception twice. In addition, the Wi-Fi connection repeatedly dropped, making it difficult for me to attend two scheduled online meetings. When I reported this the following morning, the receptionist acknowledged the problem but offered no practical solution. The room itself was clean, and I appreciate that, but these other issues significantly affected my stay.

I would be grateful if you could investigate how the noise complaint was handled and explain what steps will be taken to prevent a recurrence. Given the disruption and the failure to provide a service that was advertised, I would also consider a partial refund appropriate. I can provide my booking reference and the dates of my calls if required.

I look forward to hearing from you within the next seven days.

Yours faithfully,  
Alex Morgan

## Why this is a strong response

- **Task Achievement:** It covers all three bullet points, explains the impact, and makes a specific, proportionate request.
- **Coherence and Cohesion:** The letter moves from purpose to evidence, impact, and requested resolution in a clear sequence.
- **Lexical Resource:** Phrases such as “fell well below the standard,” “practical solution,” and “partial refund” are precise and appropriate to a formal complaint.
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** It uses a controlled mix of simple and complex sentences, including a concession (“The room itself was clean...”) and conditional/polite request forms.

**Practice note:** The response is above the 150-word minimum. This criterion-based review illustrates high-band features; it is not an official band score.
