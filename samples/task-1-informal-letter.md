# IELTS General Training Writing Task 1 — Informal Letter

## Practice prompt

A friend has invited you to stay with them for a week, but you cannot visit on the dates they suggested. Write a letter to your friend. In your letter:

- thank your friend for the invitation;
- explain why you cannot come at that time;
- suggest another time to visit and describe what you could do together.

Write at least 150 words. You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

## Model answer

Hi Maya,

Thanks so much for inviting me to stay with you next month. It would be lovely to catch up properly, especially since we have mostly exchanged messages over the past year. I was already looking forward to exploring your new neighbourhood and finally meeting your dog, Luna.

Unfortunately, I won’t be able to come during the week you suggested because I have to help my parents move into their new flat. The removal company can only deliver their furniture on those dates, and they need an extra pair of hands to unpack and organise everything. I’m disappointed to miss the original plan, but I hope you understand.

Could we move the visit to the second week of November instead? I should be free from the 10th onwards, although I can be flexible if another weekend suits you better. If the weather is decent, we could take a day trip to the coast; otherwise, I’d be just as happy to try that little Italian restaurant you told me about and spend an evening catching up at home. I can bring the board game we used to play at university, too.

Let me know what works for you. I’m sure we’ll find a date soon!

Lots of love,  
Sam

## Why this is a strong response

- **Task Achievement:** All three bullet points are covered with relevant details, including a clear alternative date and activities.
- **Coherence and Cohesion:** Each paragraph has a distinct purpose, and the proposed plans follow naturally from the explanation.
- **Lexical Resource:** The language is varied but natural for a friend, with expressions such as “catch up properly” and “an extra pair of hands.”
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** Time clauses, a conditional sentence, and varied sentence openings are used accurately without making the letter sound unnatural.

**Practice note:** The response is above the 150-word minimum. This criterion-based review illustrates high-band features; it is not an official band score.
