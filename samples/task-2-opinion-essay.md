# IELTS General Training Writing Task 2 — Opinion Essay

## Practice prompt

Some people believe that working from home benefits employees and employers, while others think that it creates more problems than it solves. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Write at least 250 words. You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

## Model answer

Working from home has become a realistic option in many occupations, prompting debate about its effects on both staff and organisations. I largely agree that remote work can benefit both groups, provided that employers establish clear expectations and do not assume that every role or employee is suited to it.

The main advantage for employees is greater control over their time. Without a daily commute, workers can use those hours for family responsibilities, exercise or rest, which may improve their well-being and allow them to concentrate more effectively. This flexibility can also widen access to employment for people who live far from major cities or have mobility difficulties. For example, a qualified specialist may be able to take a position with a company in another region without having to relocate. Employers, in turn, can recruit from a broader pool of candidates and may spend less on office space.

Nevertheless, remote work is not automatically more productive. New employees may find it harder to learn informally, while complex projects often depend on quick conversations and a shared understanding of priorities. Working from home can also blur the boundary between professional and personal life; without reasonable limits, staff may feel obliged to answer messages well into the evening. These problems are particularly likely when managers measure commitment by online availability rather than by the quality of completed work.

For this reason, organisations should treat remote work as a working arrangement that needs thoughtful design, rather than as a universal solution. Regular team meetings, clear performance goals and agreed periods when employees are not expected to respond can preserve collaboration while protecting personal time. Where practical tasks require specialist equipment or face-to-face contact, a hybrid schedule may be more suitable.

In conclusion, I believe remote work offers substantial benefits, especially through flexibility and access to talent. However, those benefits are most likely to be sustained when employers address isolation, communication and work-life boundaries deliberately.

## Why this is a strong response

- **Task Response:** The position (“largely agree”) is clear, maintained, and qualified; advantages and limitations are both developed.
- **Coherence and Cohesion:** The argument progresses from benefits to drawbacks, then to practical conditions and a direct conclusion.
- **Lexical Resource:** Topic vocabulary is precise and natural, including “broader pool of candidates,” “blur the boundary,” and “working arrangement.”
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** The essay uses concession, relative clauses, conditionals, and complex sentences with clear control.
