# Globally Influential Countries and Major Cities
## British English IPA Reference (Curated Edition)

**Scope:** This is a selective learning list, not a ranking and not a complete list of countries. Countries are included because they have notable international influence through one or more of these areas: military capability, economic scale and trade, or international tourism.

**Pronunciation key**
- IPA is an approximate **British English pronunciation** of the English country/city name, using a contemporary Southern British reference where practical.
- For place names borrowed from other languages, English pronunciation may differ from the local-language pronunciation.
- `—` means no additional city is included here; the capital is listed separately.
- “Major cities” are illustrative, internationally recognisable examples, not an official ranking.

## Notes on accuracy and interpretation

1. **Country selection is deliberately selective.** Inclusion reflects international visibility in military affairs, economic activity/trade, or tourism; it does not imply that omitted countries are unimportant.
2. **Capital status can be nuanced.** South Africa has three capitals by function; Pretoria is the executive/administrative capital. Some countries have disputed or complex political arrangements, which are not exhaustively covered in this learning list.
3. **IPA is a learning aid, not a claim about local pronunciation.** English speakers vary, and some pronunciations of foreign place names are contested or changing. For formal publication, verify each transcription against a specialist pronunciation dictionary or a native-speaker source.
4. The table avoids giving a numeric “power” score because military capability, economic influence and tourism are different measures and should not be collapsed into one ranking.
